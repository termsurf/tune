## Model

There is only experience (zusa). It is both the cause (kaza, to experience), and effect (zaka, the result of the experience). Every experience leads to a change (vita), the result of the last experience.

The experience experiences the experiences of other experiences.

Each experience implies a thing experiencing, the experience itself, the self (sufa). And also implies the other (fosa). But these are not separate from the experience, they are name for the identity of the experience.

These "other experiences" are experienced as vibes (vaba) of the connected network of experiences (zuswawiva).

Experiences have different depths of awakeness (daxwabuda). And levels of ignorance as well (bid daxa).

Experiences slowly realize they are the entire universe (luja), and move toward experiencing the infinite depth of experience. Or they may forget (nula), and be scoped to a partial perspective.

Experiences ultimately follow the way of peace (siza), but each experience must slowly figure out the path that leads to peace. Knowing how to find peace is wisdom (jaka). Wisdom means having the knowledge through experience of how to follow the good path. The foolish (koja) traverse the vibes with little wisdom.

The good (tafa) is the experience toward the center, toward peace. The bad (futa) is the experience toward the extremes, pleasure and pain.

Too much pleasure for one leads to pain for another. The path to peace is the center.

Awareness (duba) is constantly created (bama), persisted (vixa), and destroyed (xiva), but experience never goes away. Awareness is constantly gained (ziga), and lost (guza).

As such, experience rises and falls. Sometimes it is rich (ripa), and sometimes, poor (pora). Sometimes it is wise, sometimes, foolish.

Humans (drama) are one type of experience out of the flow (jula) of experiences in the universe. Humans are an aggregate (kana) of experiences, integrating multiple organs (laga), on top of simpler experiences, on top of simpler experiences, etc..

---

To pray means to align (moka) your intentions (kiva) with the universe.

How does this work?

Focus your soul (sola) on simulating (liqa) the state locally (ruva).

Connect to the divine field (davyacuva) and stream (jili) your soul into it.

The soul is the state of your experiential body. The accumulation of past experiences.

The divine field is an everywhere-permeating mesh of linked experiences.

It has been structured and organized by the most wise force.

Beings (baqa) can be pure experience (pit zusa), or have a physical manifestation (dak vila). Nothing is pure physical.

You only have a soul when you begin to separate yourself from the oneness. Isolating yourself into a self.

God is a richer experience, unaffected significantly by all that happens within.
