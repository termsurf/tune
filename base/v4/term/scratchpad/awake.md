# The `d` `b` quartet

A mirror pair makes exactly four words, and `d b` makes these four. They
are a closed set, so they have to be settled together.

```text
        i               u
  d-    dib             dub
  b-    bid             bud
```

## Where it stood

```text
dib   connect      the odd one out, nothing to do with the other three
dub   aware
bid   ignorant
bud   awake
```

**`dub` aware and `bud` awake were near-synonyms**, which wasted one of
only four slots, and `dib` connect had no relation to the other three.
So the quartet carried two and a half meanings across four words.

## Why the proposal is structurally right

```text
*   dib coud be ignorance, and bud enlightenment (since u is like
    inner depth)
```

**Both diagonals are reversal pairs**, which is the rule in
`note/tune/pipeline/philosophy.md` principle 3.

```text
dib  <->  bud        d i b  /  b u d      reversed, vowel i to u
bid  <->  dub        b i d  /  d u b      reversed, vowel i to u
```

This was the one opposite pair in the lexicon that was **not** a
consonant reversal. `bud` and `bid` differed by vowel alone, and every
other opposite pair reverses. Taking the diagonals fixes that, and the
quartet becomes two opposite pairs instead of one pair plus two
leftovers.

The vowel then does the work you described: `i` is the surface and the
outside, `u` is the inner depth. Ignorance is the surface, enlightenment
is the depth.

## The second diagonal

```text
*   dub could be to connect, perhaps.
```

That keeps `connect` inside the quartet instead of displacing it, which
is the better outcome: `connect` had nowhere obvious to go, and `lik`
link is the only nearby form.

**Which leaves `bid` as the one open slot**, and the rule says what it
has to be: **the opposite of connect.**

| candidate | note |
| :--- | :--- |
| cut, sever | the clean opposite, and it has no word yet |
| disconnect | exact, but `nub` already means separate |
| break | wider than needed |

**Check `nub` before choosing.** `nub` is `separate` and it carries
`suf` = `nub zus`, separate experience, so it is load-bearing in the
ontology. If `nub` is the general separation, then `bid` is the specific
undoing of a connection, and the two can coexist. If they cannot, `bid`
should be something else and the quartet keeps one empty slot rather
than forcing a word in, per principle 5.

## What this costs

```text
dib   connect    ->  ignorance        meaning moves to dub
dub   aware      ->  connect          "aware" merges into bud
bud   awake      ->  enlightenment    absorbs awake and aware
bid   ignorant   ->  open             freed
```

**No meaning is lost.** `aware` and `awake` were the same word twice, and
`ignorant` becomes `dib` ignorance as a noun. One slot is freed rather
than spent, which is the opposite of what a displacement usually costs.

`connection` is one of the four axioms in `philosophy.md`, so moving it
inside the same mirror quartet rather than out of it keeps the axiom on
a structurally marked form.
