# The symmetric words

From the first table at <https://tune.surf/term/action>. Twenty four
words, and **they are the most important three letter words in the
language** because of what they look like rather than what they sound
like.

## They are symmetric in Tone script

Written in Tone, each of these reads the same forwards and backwards.
The glyph for the closing consonant is the **mirror image** of the glyph
for the opening one, and the vowel in the middle is a glyph that mirrors
onto itself.

```text
puk      ㄱ u ㄍ        the p and the k face each other
tiv      ≥ w ≤
kip      ㄍ w ㄱ
him      M w M
```

So the word is a shape with an axis down the middle. That is a property
no other words have, and it makes these the natural set for the most
fundamental actions.

## The eight mirror pairs

| | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `s` `z` | `n` `q` | `d` `b` |
| `p` `k` | `t` `v` | `j` `l` |
| `h` `m` | `w` `g` | |

Sixteen of the twenty two consonants pair off. The six left over are
`f` `x` `c` `C` `r` `y`, which pair with nothing and so cannot make a
symmetric word.

## Four words per pair, where the rules allow

A mirror pair `C1 C2` makes four words: each consonant opening, each
vowel between.

```text
d b   ->   dib   dub   bid   bud      all four
p k   ->   pik   puk   kip   kup      all four
t v   ->   tiv   tuv   vit   vut      all four
j l   ->   jil   jul   lij   luj      only two, see below
n q   ->   niq   nuq                  q cannot open
h m   ->   him   hum                  h cannot close
w g   ->   wig   wug                  w cannot close
s z   ->   siz   zus                  two of the four are listed
```

**The vowel is always `i` or `u`.** Those are the two arms of the
upright axis, and they are the two vowels whose glyphs are symmetric.
`i` is the bright, outer, upper one and `u` the dark, inner, lower one,
so each pair of words is one idea taken two ways.

```text
tuv  input        vut  be
kip  tune         kup  tone
him  chime        hum  urge
wig  mesh         wug  emanate
```

## Two problems to settle

### `jil` and `jul` are not legal v4 words

Both are killed by the blurred rhyme rule: **a liquid closes only on
`a` or `o`**, and `il` and `ul` are on the banned list. So the `j` `l`
pair can only make `lij` and `luj`, and loses half its set.

That is a direct conflict between two good rules. The rhyme rule exists
because `bil` cannot be held apart from `bi`, and the symmetry exists
because these are the foundational words. One of them has to give for
this pair.

**Options.** Let `j` `l` be a two rather than a four and accept the
gap. Or exempt a symmetric word from the rhyme rule, which is narrow
and defensible since the set is closed and small. Or drop `stream` and
`jewel` to other words and lose the symmetry for them.

### The lexicon and the site disagree on ten meanings

`tune.csv` and the page say different things for nearly half the table.

| word | `tune.csv` | the site |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `siz` | peace | experience |
| `zus` | experience | do |
| `niq` | omniscient | flow |
| `dib` | connect | perfect |
| `bid` | ignorant | connect |
| `bud` | awake | awaken |
| `pik` | master | find |
| `tiv` | conscious | absorb |
| `vit` | information | output |
| `him` | nothing | chime |
| `luj` | logic | drive |

Ten agree, eleven differ, and `puk` and `vut` are unassigned in the
lexicon.

**The site is probably newer.** But this is a decision rather than a
merge: `siz` and `zus` have swapped between the two, which is the sort
of thing that happens when a set is reworked, and taking one source
wholesale is safer than taking it row by row.

## What it would take to adopt the site's version

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| `puk` | free, take it |
| `vut` | legal but not picked, add to the required set |
| `jil` `jul` | not legal, need a rule decision |
| eleven others | already mean something else, need reassigning |

The eleven displaced meanings would each need a new word, and several
are themselves fundamental: `peace`, `conscious`, `information`,
`logic`, `master`.
