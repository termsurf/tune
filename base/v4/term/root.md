## This note settles the `-ul` question

The constraints section below is the clearest statement anywhere of what
is actually banned:

> no `-ur` endings (disliked). Plus the phonotactic bans already in the
> generator: no `-el/-il/-er/-ir` finals, no `q`-initial, no `h/w/y`
> finals.

**`-ul` is not on either list.** It is not a phonotactic ban and it is
not a disliked sound. It was added to `BAD_RHYME` on the argument that a
rounded back vowel and a liquid share a tongue gesture, which is a
reasonable thought and is not what this note says.

That matters because `-ul` is doing real damage:

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| `tul` | it is a tool, a constructor |
| `gul` | that, a constructor |
| `jul` | jewel, one of the symmetric words |

Three of the six words lost to the rhyme rule are lost to `-ul` alone,
and **dropping it gives back 252 words** across the language, from 6,233
to 6,485.

```text
il el ir er ul ur      6,233     now
il el ir er    ur      6,485     +252, and tul gul jul come back
il el ir er            6,737     +504, but ur is disliked
```

**The middle row looks right.** It matches what this note says, keeps
`-ur` out because the sound is disliked, and recovers three wanted
words. `mir` and `jil` stay illegal either way, since `-ir` and `-il`
are genuine bans.

Nothing has been changed yet. This is the evidence for the decision.

## What this note is

64 base terms with ideal derivations from the sounds in
[`../system/sound.md`](../system/sound.md). The centre of the
universe-model, in words. Built to be felt, checked, and refined.

(Named `-derived` to sit beside the older `base-terms.md`, which it does not
replace.)

These are **roots** (pre-grammatical). Each becomes a thing with `-a`, an action
with `-i`, a feature with `-u`, and so on. `man` -> `mana` (a mind), `mani` (to
think), `manu` (mental).

**Confidence markers:**

- **★** = the user's own carefully hand-made words (the Tone-script set, plus the
  `man` anchor). Highest confidence. Derivations here are *read off* what you
  already made.
- **◆** = newly derived here, strong fit.
- **○** = provisional, honoring an older root with a fresh derivation. Most likely
  to change.

Read each as `root — meaning | derivation`.

**Sound constraints (user preferences):** no `-ur` endings (disliked). (Plus the
phonotactic bans already in the generator: no `-el/-il/-er/-ir` finals, no
`q`-initial, no `h/w/y` finals.)

## How to read the feel

Speak each root aloud, then its forms (`-a` thing, `-i` action, `-u` feature).
Ask: does the sound *enact* the meaning. Does it feel inevitable, like `man`. Does
it sit right in the cosmology.

Where it does, bless it (it joins the gold set). Where it strains, mark it and
tell me which sound or reading feels off. The `○` terms are the first to expect
changes. The `★` terms are your own, shown here only to make their derivations
explicit and confirm they still hold under the final sound table.

From your feedback I will adjust the sound meanings (`sound-meanings.md`) and
re-derive, and we iterate.
