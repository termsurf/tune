# Seven

**Vowel pattern: `i o u e u o i` — around to the left arm, then back
the way it came.**

```text
      i           i --> o
   e  a  o        ^     |
      u           e <-- u   then retrace
```

Up, right, down, left, then down, right, up again. It walks the four
directions to the far side and **returns along the same path**, so it
is a palindrome and the turn happens on the left arm, which is said
once.

Seven never touches the centre. It is a four that goes out and comes
back rather than closing the loop.

## What seven is for

Seven is the first size with **no good structural reason to exist**. It
is not a power of two, not a product of small factors, not a count of
axes or dimensions. 7 is prime and awkward: it divides nothing evenly.

And yet it is everywhere in human systems. That is the point of seven:
**it is the size of things people keep rather than things the world
has.** A week is seven because somebody decided; a rainbow is seven
because Newton wanted it to match the notes.

So seven's list is almost entirely conventional, and it is the size
where that matters least, because those conventions are deeply kept.

## Fundamental, or nearly

| domain | the seven | how clean |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SI base units | metre · kilogram · second · ampere · kelvin · mole · candela | seven by definition, and the definition is careful |
| frieze groups | seven ways to repeat a pattern along a line | provably exactly seven |
| bridges of Königsberg | seven, and the proof they cannot be walked | the birth of graph theory |
| crystal systems | cubic · tetragonal · orthorhombic · hexagonal · trigonal · monoclinic · triclinic | exactly seven lattice shapes |

**The frieze groups and the crystal systems are the real sevens.** Both
are counted, not chosen, and neither could have come out differently.

## The diatonic seven

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| notes | C · D · E · F · G · A · B |
| intervals | tone tone semitone tone tone tone semitone |

This sits between fundamental and conventional. Seven is not arbitrary:
it falls out of stacking perfect fifths and folding them into an
octave, and the near-closure at seven is a fact about the ratio 3:2,
not a human choice. But **which** seven, and the choice of twelve
underneath it, is ours.

Worth treating as conventional with a real reason under it.

## Conventional

| domain | the seven |
| :--- | :--- |
| the week | Monday through Sunday |
| the rainbow | red orange yellow green blue indigo violet |
| classical planets | Sun Moon Mercury Venus Mars Jupiter Saturn |
| the deadly sins | seven |
| the seas | seven |
| the wonders | seven |
| chakras | seven |
| a phone number, once | seven digits, chosen for memory |

**The rainbow seven is a good cautionary case.** There is no seventh
colour in the spectrum: indigo was added to make the count match the
notes and the planets. A Tune colour system should use [3](3.md) for
the light primaries or [16](16.md) for a full set, and not seven.

## Advice

**Do not build a seven unless the thing really is seven.** The size has
no structure to lend, the vowel path is a there-and-back rather than a
shape, and most sevens in the world are sixes or eights that were
rounded for a reason that no longer applies.

The week is the exception, and it is worth having because people live
in it.
